# Licenses

## Game art

The ship, projectile, explosion, fire, title and menu-background PNGs in `public/assets` are from Kenney's pirate and UI packs (https://kenney.nl). Kenney assets are published under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The tile sheets and the UI sprite atlas described in the challenge were not part of this workspace. Water and islands are drawn in code. Their shapes match the collision circles.

## Sound

`public/assets/sounds/*.wav` are original short clips generated by `scripts/make-sounds.mjs`. They are dedicated to the public domain (CC0). The challenge's original WAV pack was not in the workspace.

## Fonts

The UI uses `system-ui`. No font files are bundled.

## Code dependencies

Third-party packages keep the licenses in `node_modules` and are declared in `package.json`. The notable ones are React, PixiJS, TanStack Query, Axios, MSW and Playwright.
